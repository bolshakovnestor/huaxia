Good to know

  • 西安Xī'ān (Xi'an), ancient 长安Cháng'ān (Chang'an), was the capital of the Han and Tang and the starting point of the Silk Road.
  • In English it is spelled Shaanxi (double a) to distinguish it from Shanxi.

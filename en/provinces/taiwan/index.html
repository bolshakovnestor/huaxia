Taiwan (disputed status) · 台湾 TáiwānEast China · 华东
Since 1949 the island has been governed by the Republic of China (中华民国Zhōnghuá Mínguó (Republic of China)). The PRC regards Taiwan as its province; its international status remains disputed.

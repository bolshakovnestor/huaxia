汉语与汉字
Hànyǔ yǔ Hànzì

Language and characters

Chinese is a tonal language, and its script is one of the oldest continuously used writing systems in the world.

What the language is called

Three names

汉语Hànyǔ (“language of the Han”), 中文Zhōngwén (Chinese (mainly written)), 普通话Pǔtōnghuà (standard Mandarin).

Dialects

There are major 方言fāngyán (dialect) groups: 粤语Yuèyǔ (Cantonese), 吴语Wúyǔ (Wu (Shanghai)), 闽语Mǐnyǔ (Min (Fujian, Taiwan)) and others. Speakers of different groups often can't understand each other's speech, but they read the same characters.

Tones

Four tones + the neutral tone

The same syllable with different tones gives different words: 妈mā (mother) (1st, level), 麻má (hemp) (2nd, rising), 马mǎ (horse) (3rd, falling-rising), 骂mà (to scold) (4th, falling), 吗ma (question particle) (neutral). Tap the buttons below to listen.

Pinyin

Romanisation

拼音pīnyīn (“spelling sounds”) was officially adopted in the PRC in 1958 and became the international standard ISO 7098 in 1982. Tones are marked with diacritics: ā á ǎ à.

Characters

The oldest script

甲骨文jiǎgǔwén (writing on bones and shells) of the Shang era (≈1250–1050 BC) was found at Anyang, Henan province.

How characters are formed

象形xiàngxíng (pictograms): 山 “mountain”, 木 “tree”. 会意huìyì (compound ideograms): 休 “to rest” = person 亻 + tree 木. 形声xíngshēng (phono-semantic compounds) (meaning component + sound component) — the vast majority of characters, e.g. 妈 = 女 “woman” + 马 mǎ (sound).

Radicals

A 部首bùshǒu (radical) is the graphic component used to order characters in dictionaries: 氵 “water”, 木 “tree”, 口 “mouth”, 亻 “person”.

How many characters you need

The 康熙字典Kāngxī Zìdiǎn (Kangxi Dictionary) (1716) lists over 47,000 characters, but about 3,000 are enough to read a newspaper fluently.

Simplified and traditional

简体字jiǎntǐzì (simplified) — mainland China, Singapore; 繁体字fántǐzì (traditional) — Hong Kong, Macau, Taiwan. Example: 龙 / 龍 “dragon”, 马 / 馬 “horse”.

Exams

HSK

The 汉语水平考试Hànyǔ Shuǐpíng Kǎoshì (Chinese Proficiency Test) is the international certificate for non-native speakers.

生肖与象征
shēngxiào yǔ xiàngzhēng

Zodiac and symbols

Dragon, phoenix, the colour red, “lucky” and “unlucky” numbers — symbolism runs through everyday life.

The Chinese zodiac 生肖

12 animals

A 12-year cycle: 鼠shǔ (Rat), 牛niú (Ox), 虎hǔ (Tiger), 兔tù (Rabbit), 龙lóng (Dragon), 蛇shé (Snake), 马mǎ (Horse), 羊yáng (Goat/Sheep), 猴hóu (Monkey), 鸡jī (Rooster), 狗gǒu (Dog), 猪zhū (Pig). 2026 is the year of the Horse (马年mǎ nián (year of the Horse)). Find your animal below!

Five elements

五行wǔxíng (the five elements): 木mù (wood), 火huǒ (fire), 土tǔ (earth), 金jīn (metal), 水shuǐ (water). Combined with the 12 animals they form a 60-year cycle.

Mythical creatures

Dragon

The 龙lóng (dragon), unlike its European cousin, is a benevolent force that commands water and rain; a symbol of the emperor.

Phoenix

The 凤凰fènghuáng (phoenix) symbolises harmony and the empress; “dragon and phoenix” together stand for a happy marriage.

Qilin

The 麒麟qílín (qilin) is an auspicious creature heralding a wise ruler.

Colours

Red

红色hóngsè (red) — happiness, celebration, luck; the colour of weddings.

Yellow

黄色huángsè (yellow) — the emperor's colour; the roofs of the Forbidden City are covered with yellow tiles.

White

白色báisè (white) — traditionally the colour of mourning.

Numbers

8 — lucky

八bā (eight) sounds like 发fā (to prosper). The Beijing Olympics opened on 08.08.2008 at 8:08 pm.

4 — unlucky

四sì (four) sounds similar to 死sǐ (death); buildings often have no 4th floor.

6 and 9

六liù (six) — “smooth, successful” (六六大顺liù liù dà shùn (“everything goes smoothly”)); 九jiǔ (nine) sounds like 久jiǔ (long-lasting).

China today: the state

Key facts about the 中华人民共和国Zhōnghuá Rénmín Gònghéguó (People's Republic of China): symbols, government, population and geography.

Key facts

Capital and founding date

The capital is 北京Běijīng (Beijing). The PRC was proclaimed on 1 October 1949; the day is celebrated as 国庆节Guóqìngjié (National Day).

Territory

About 9.6 million km² — one of the largest countries in the world. The whole country runs on a single time, 北京时间Běijīng shíjiān (Beijing time) (UTC+8).

Population

The 2020 census counted 1,411,778,724 people (excluding Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan). The 汉族Hànzú (Han people) make up ≈91.1%.

Language

The national language is 普通话Pǔtōnghuà (Putonghua, “common speech”), written in 简体字jiǎntǐzì (simplified characters).

Currency

人民币Rénmínbì (Renminbi, “the people's currency”); the basic unit is the 元yuán (yuan): 1 yuan = 10 角jiǎo (jiao) = 100 分fēn (fen).

Ethnic groups

56 民族mínzú (ethnic groups) are officially recognised. The largest minorities include the 壮族Zhuàngzú (Zhuang), 维吾尔族Wéiwú'ěrzú (Uyghurs), 回族Huízú (Hui), 苗族Miáozú (Miao) and 满族Mǎnzú (Manchu).

State symbols

Flag

The 五星红旗Wǔxīng Hóngqí (“Five-starred Red Flag”): a red field with one large yellow star and four smaller ones.

Anthem

义勇军进行曲Yìyǒngjūn Jìnxíngqǔ (“March of the Volunteers”) — music by 聂耳Niè Ěr (Nie Er), lyrics by 田汉Tián Hàn (Tian Han) (1935).

Emblem

The 国徽guóhuī (national emblem) shows the 天安门Tiān'ānmén (Tiananmen, “Gate of Heavenly Peace”) beneath five stars, framed by ears of grain and a cogwheel.

Political system

Legislature

The 全国人民代表大会Quánguó Rénmín Dàibiǎo Dàhuì (National People's Congress (NPC)) is, under the Constitution, the highest organ of state power.

Government

The 国务院Guówùyuàn (State Council) is the central government, headed by the 总理zǒnglǐ (premier). The head of state is the 国家主席guójiā zhǔxí (President of the PRC).

Ruling party

The Constitution establishes the leading role of the 中国共产党Zhōngguó Gòngchǎndǎng (Communist Party of China).

Administrative divisions

34 provincial-level divisions (in the PRC's official position)

23 省shěng (provinces) (including Taiwan, which the PRC does not control), 5 自治区zìzhìqū (autonomous regions), 4 直辖市zhíxiáshì (municipalities) and 2 特别行政区tèbié xíngzhèngqū (special administrative regions). See the interactive map for details.

Geography

Highest point

珠穆朗玛峰Zhūmùlǎngmǎ Fēng (Qomolangma (Mount Everest)) — 8,848.86 m (joint China–Nepal measurement, 2020).

Great rivers

The 长江Cháng Jiāng (Yangtze, “Long River”) (~6,300 km, the longest in Asia) and the 黄河Huáng Hé (Yellow River) (~5,460 km), called “the mother of Chinese civilisation”.

Neighbours

China shares land borders with 14 countries — together with Russia, the most in the world.
